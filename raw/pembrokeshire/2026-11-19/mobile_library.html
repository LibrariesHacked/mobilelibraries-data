About us 

Pembrokeshire Libraries runs a mobile Library service which covers many parts of the County.  Every stop on each route will be visited once every four weeks, at the same time.

The mobile features a range of fiction, non-fiction, large print and audio books, a small number of books for children and access to the internet.

You can also reserve items that are not currently on the mobile.

If you are already using a community library, you can use your membership on board the mobile.

Items can be borrowed for four weeks, after which you can renew them either, by phone, online, or at the mobile library.

There are no fines and charges for books returned late on the mobile library, but it is helpful to other library users if you bring them back on time.

The mobile has lift access for users with mobility problems.
