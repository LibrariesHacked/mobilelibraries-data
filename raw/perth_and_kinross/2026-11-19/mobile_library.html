Mobile Library

The mobile library is for people who might not be able to get to their local library. It travels over 50,000 miles a year, taking books and resources to the rural parts of Perth and Kinross, not serviced by a community library building

Timetables

View scheduled stops for each day (all times subject to traffic and weather conditions). Stops and timings may be subject to change. Occasionally vehicle failure or bad weather may cause the service to be delayed or withdrawn. If you think your service may have been affected call 01738 444949. Find out more information about our library services here, visit or telephone 01738 444949.
