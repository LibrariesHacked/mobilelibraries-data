Mobile library

Our brand‑new Mobile Library is visiting communities across Midlothian.

You are welcome to step on board, browse the shelves and borrow books as usual. You can also reserve books online using the library catalogue and collect them from any Mobile Library stop.

Full list of services:

  • Browse and borrow books
  • Reserve and collect items
  • Citizens Advice Digital Information Point (VERA)
  • Pick up free period products
  • Collect hearing aid batteries
  • National Entitlement Card help and support

Routes and timetables

Our service runs on a two-week schedule.
