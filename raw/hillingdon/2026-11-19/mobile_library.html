Mobile library

Our mobile library offers a valuable service, particularly to residents who cannot visit their local library.

Access a range of books in ordinary and large print, talking books on CD, children's books, and free wifi with this service, which visits roadside sites, schools, nurseries, day centres, residential and sheltered homes.

Mobile library timetable

Use our timetable below to find your nearest mobile library stop and when we'll be visiting.

There will be no mobile library service from Tuesday 17 to Friday 20 November. We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.

Please note:

  • Our mobile library will park either in the car park of the premises or near the main entrance.
  • Our timetables below are subject to change and will be reviewed regularly.
