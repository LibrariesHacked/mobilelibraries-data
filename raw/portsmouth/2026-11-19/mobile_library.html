Mobile Library

Portsmouth Library Service has a great mobile library with books, talking books and information for all ages and interests

All are welcome – bring your existing library card or join for the first time.

The Mobile Library stops at a variety of locations across the city on a weekly basis; see the timetable section for details.

For mobile library enquiries, email mobile.library@portsmouthcc.gov.uk.

Opening Hours

  • Mondays
    9.30am-12.30pm: Drayton Lane / Havant Road, 1.30pm-4.30pm: Henderson/Ferry Road Eastney
  • Tuesdays
    (9.30am-12.30pm: Individual deliveries) 1.30pm-4.30pm: top of the Green, opposite St Michael’s Church, Paulsgrove
  • Wednesdays
    9.30am-12.30pm: London Road (opposite Amberley Road), 1.30pm-4.30pm: Mayfield Road / Copnor Road
  • Thursdays
    9.30am-12.30pm: Sainsbury’s Farlington, 1.30pm-4.30pm: Morrisons Anchorage Park
  • Fridays
    9.30am-12.30pm: Canoe Lake, 1.30pm-4.30pm: Canoe Lake
