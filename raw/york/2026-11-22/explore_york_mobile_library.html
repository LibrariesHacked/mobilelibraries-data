About Us

Our mobile library offers access to a range of Explore services. You can browse, borrow and return books, as well as place and collect reservations. We have a large selection of audio and large print books on board. You can also get your Library and York Cards. We visit each stop every two weeks, covering the villages and outskirts of York. All our libraries are accessible and there is a lift for wheelchair access on our mobile library.

If you would like to have some books personally selected for you then please contact Dean at mobile@exploreyork.org.uk  Just let him know what genres, authors and format (large print, audio, paperback, hardback) you prefer and he will select some titles based on the information you provide. He will then be in touch to arrange the time for you to collect your books and return any that you have out on loan.
