Mobile routes

To download the latest mobile library routes schedule and diary, please click here.

To find out when your stop is visited, you will need to find out which week your stop is in. Look at the routes schedule and search for your stop. You will then be able to see which is your week, eg Chillerton appears in the Week 1 route.

Then refer to the dates table.  This will tell you which weeks the route you have selected will be served by the mobile library.

If for any reason the mobile is unable to call on a specific date we will contact customers by telephone to inform them of any problems.
